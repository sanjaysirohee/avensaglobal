Australia Tourist Visa (Subclass 600)

Australia is one of the most preferred travel destinations for vacations, family visits, and short-term business activities. If you are planning to visit Australia for tourism or personal purposes, you must apply for an Australia Tourist Visa (Subclass 600). At Avensa Global, we provide expert guidance to help you successfully apply for an Australia Tourist Visa, ensuring accuracy, compliance, and timely processing.

What Activities Are Allowed on an Australia Tourist Visa?

The Subclass 600 – Visitor Visa allows you to visit Australia for:

  • Holiday, vacation, and recreational travel
  • Visiting friends, relatives, or family members
  • Business visitor activities such as conferences, meetings, or negotiations
  • Short-term informal study or training (up to 3 months)

Activities NOT Allowed on an Australia Tourist Visa

  • Work in Australia, even short-term paid work
  • Provide services to Australian businesses
  • Sell goods or services to the public
  • Engage in activities not permitted under Subclass 600

Eligibility Criteria for Australia Tourist Visa (Subclass 600)

To apply for an Australian Tourist Visa, the applicant must:

  • Prove intent to leave Australia before visa expiry
  • Demonstrate strong ties to their home country (India)
  • Clearly state a valid purpose of visit
  • Show sufficient funds to support their stay and travel
  • Meet health requirements
  • Provide character clearance
  • Submit details of previous international travel
  • Declare any earlier Australian visa applications
  • Prove compliance with visa conditions, if previously visited Australia

How to Prove Strong Ties to Home Country?

Visa officers must be convinced that the applicant will return to India. This can be proven by submitting:

  • Employment details and approved leave letter
  • Proof of admission for students
  • Income proof, assets, or property in India
  • Family ties and responsibilities in India
  • Business ownership or professional commitments
