What Is a Canada Study Permit?

A Study Permit is an official document issued by Canadian immigration authorities that allows eligible foreign nationals to study at a Designated Learning Institution (DLI) in Canada. Most international students also need a visitor visa (TRV) or an Electronic Travel Authorization (eTA), depending on their nationality, to travel to Canada.

APPLICATION REQUIREMENTS

Basic Eligibility Criteria

To apply for a Canada Study Permit, applicants generally need to:

  • Have an acceptance letter from a Designated Learning Institution (DLI).
  • Meet the applicable financial requirements.
  • Provide the required supporting documents.
  • Complete a medical examination if required.
  • Satisfy immigration requirements, including temporary resident conditions.
